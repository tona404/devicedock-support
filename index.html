Support

Need help with DeviceDock? Email crumbdev.team@gmail.com. Include your macOS version and the action you were trying to complete.

Android devices

Install Android SDK Platform Tools and the Android Emulator. For wireless pairing, enable Wireless debugging on Android 11 or later and place the Mac and device on the same local network.

Apple simulators

Install Xcode and at least one simulator runtime. DeviceDock uses the development tools already installed on your Mac.

System requirements

  • macOS 13 or later
  • Xcode for Apple simulator features
  • Android SDK Emulator and Platform Tools for Android features

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 26, 2026

DeviceDock does not collect, sell, share, or transmit personal data to the developer or to advertising or analytics services.

Local device information

The app reads device names, identifiers, runtime versions, connection status, and local network discovery results from developer tools installed on your Mac. This information is processed locally to display and manage your devices.

Wireless pairing

Android pairing addresses and pairing codes are sent directly to the locally installed Android Debug Bridge. DeviceDock does not store pairing codes.

Network access

Local network access is used only to discover and connect to development devices you choose to manage. DeviceDock does not operate a remote data-collection service.
